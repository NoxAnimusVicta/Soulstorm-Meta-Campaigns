## Purpose

Record Jake's balance feedback during the Atreus campaign for a combined assessment after the campaign ends. These notes do not amend the active rules. Apply a mid-campaign rule change only when Jake explicitly approves it.

## Recording procedure

- Append each balance observation Jake provides with a stable ID, date and campaign Cycle.
- Preserve his meaning and distinguish his observations and suggestions from assistant analysis.
- Record the relevant battle, faction, trait, construction or resource situation where known. Link to campaign evidence when available; leave unknown details explicitly unconfirmed.
- Separate a suspected balance issue from a confirmed rules or bookkeeping error. Note when an error may have affected the observation.
- Keep follow-up evidence under the original entry rather than duplicating it. Record counterexamples as well as supporting examples.
- Do not turn an observation into a rule change or launch simulations automatically.
- At campaign end, review the entries together, including interactions between proposed changes, before recommending a balance package.
