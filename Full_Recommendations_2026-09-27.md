# Full recommendations — 27 September 2026

Recommendation document only. No rules or bots changed, no new campaigns launched, no publication. Reconciles original Campaign Notes B01–B22, the 26 September reconciliation and construction review, the focused siege continuations, and the completed construction-choice study and full inspection. Historical studies have different rules and must not be pooled as one sample.

## What the latest comparison establishes

The latest study contains 336 games, 168 per arm, with 304,486 replay-validated orders and zero errors. Both arms use identical approved rules; only purchasing decisions differ. Median duration changes 62 to 58 Cycles; 50–100-Cycle finishes 108 to 109 of168; no game remains unresolved at200. Supply deficit exposure rises2.10% to2.47%, Manpower2.72% to3.65%. These results do not justify adopting the new purchaser unchanged.

Full inspection identifies a real start/continuation inconsistency, missing combat-probability and concentration value, and increased defensive resource pressure. It does not establish that all additional shortages are caused by construction spending. Defensive Manpower losses total38,246 versus38,203, but ground-defence Manpower deficit entries rise301 to368: timing, distribution and buffers matter. The longest inspected wars feature repeated captures and counterattacks rather than global inactivity.

## 1. Construction decisions: correct four specific faults

**Completion planning.** Before starting a project, schedule its remaining paid stages and any necessary host repair alongside existing projects and foreseeable emergency actions. Record the intended operation and useful follow-on targets. Use that same operation horizon when deciding whether to continue. A target falling sooner must not automatically erase all value; losing the relevant front can legitimately cancel the plan. Compare remaining costs, not sunk expenditure. Forecast only owned income and feasible actions, not assumed future conquest.

**Actual force packages.** Compare constructions with affordable ordinary fleets using the group that can reach and participate in the operation. Preserve home defence and other already-committed actions. Value victory probability, damage, expected losses, arrival time, upkeep and commitment costs separately. Carrier changes odds and commitment; Siege Platform changes damage; Flagship changes concentration and exposure to per-fleet losses. They are not interchangeable damage purchases.

**Threat-sensitive reserves.** Retain resources for a credible imminent defensive settlement and the next Logistics shortfall when an enemy can threaten a holding. Account for defensive Manpower as well as Supply. Do not reserve for every hypothetical enemy or prohibit an intentional decisive attack. These are bot preferences, not new player restrictions or extra costs.

**Construction scheduling.** Compare starting, continuing, repairing and abandoning in one decision. A positive new purchase should not bypass a more valuable continuation. Preserve emergency responses. Do not impose a fixed number of concurrent projects or require completing an obsolete project.

Evidence:294 unfinished Bays/Platforms were destroyed by storms with surviving hosts;244 were older than their first Cycle. A saved Bay started atCycle4 is rejected for continuation atCycle5 because its current target no longer requires bonus damage. This warrants correcting scheduling before weakening storms or granting unfinished benefits.

## 2. Construction rules: retain the approved package

Keep Minor costs3 per stage, Major5; economic yields3/6 and7/14; Carrier10/20 ground strength; Siege Platform3/6 damage; Regenerative Fortifications3/6 eligible recovery; Minor Landing Zones; and surviving completed Fleet Construction base effects. Keep one holding slot, free built-in Capital yard, upgrade continuity and transferred fleet constructions. Add no fleet/system cap.

The next functional comparisons must cover the catalogue, not force equal popularity:

| Role | Decision comparison |
|---|---|
| Strength and concentration | Assault Cruiser/Flagship/Carrier versus ordinary fleet, including Manpower, upkeep, build delay and per-fleet naval loss |
| Siege damage | Assault Boats/Bay/Platform versus stronger assault group, including odds and defender recovery |
| Resource protection | Militia versus Academy/Training; Transport versus replenishment; Salvage Wing versus Forge, using avoided or realised losses/income |
| Local defence | Cannons/Bunker/Shield/Network/regeneration versus Defend or interception, including economic slot and a feasible attacker counter |
| Operations | Yard/Grand Yard/Tender/System Repair/Anchorage/Scout/Transit versus actual movement, repair and action delays |
| Expansion | Consolidation/Void Station versus improving existing holdings, including extra slot, fallback and income |

Prioritise Militia under the measured defensive-Manpower pressure, Carrier/Flagship under the proven valuation omission, and storm protection/repair against prolonged project exposure. These are priorities for selecting useful situations, not instructions to force those purchases. Eighteen of33 profiles starting is not catalogue qualification. No further numerical construction buff is recommended from these data alone.

## 3. Ground assault, naval battle and bombardment

Retain all current route rules and prices. Do not add automatic orbital losses to ground assaults or nerf bombardment because of action counts. The intended choices are: ground-first for affordable capture/Planet Fall pressure; naval-first when enemy fleets or orbital protection materially prevent the operation; bombardment when certainty and avoiding Manpower commitment justify extra Supply and delayed capture.

Compare complete capture sequences from the same positions, not one action. Include Dread costs, cannon fire, construction suppression, hostile fleets, MP returns, Planet Fall, defender repair and events. Required situations include an exposed low-defence holding, strong garrison with weak orbital cover, contested orbit, an active defence station, a Fortification Capital, and low-Manpower/high-Supply attacker.

For entrenched sieges, retain adaptive suppression, concentration, forward repair and pressure on another holding. The focused continuations showed rigid target fixation losing territory; adaptive play disabled the station in one seed. Do not make every defended Capital capturable by a small force, but do not excuse a position with no feasible counter as merely a bot problem.

## 4. Resources and deficits

Retain present income, fleet costs, no-voluntary-zero rule, forced defensive settlement and three-action recovery. No additional Manpower charge or global defensive-cost reduction now. Flat-damage constructions increasing the AI defender's incoming-damage-based commitment is current behaviour, not an implementation defect; the latest test did not isolate whether it needs rebalance. Preserve it while correcting reserve and construction decisions, and report it explicitly in the next comparison.

Measure deficit entries by source and duration, not only aggregate exposure. Separate investment before attack, enemy losses, events, Logistics and loss of income-producing territory. Keep Supply and Manpower distinct; equal deficit frequency is not the objective.

## 6. Minor factions, events and campaign duration

Keep ordinary versus prize fleet/resource formulas pending a separate encounter breakdown. Ordinary Minors are intended to be expansion prey; prize Capitals should cost more to conquer. Report first-capture time, failed attempts and resources/strength spent separately, so Major-versus-Major outcomes cannot conceal excessive Minor resistance.

Keep storms and Integrity destruction for now: interruption is intended and the observed continuation fault exaggerates exposure. Measure immediate damage, active effect loss and repair/build delay separately, including Transit protection. Do not add delayed travel.

Keep the50–100Cycle target and permit active long wars. About65% of latest candidate games fall in range and30% below50, so the pace is not universally on target. Inspect early finishes for uneven map opportunities or runaway opponents before applying a global slowdown. No artificial victory deadline or defence nerf to erase long outliers.

## 7. Documentation and player-facing work

Prepare one consolidated current draft, retaining historical versions. Replace stale body rules for costs/yields, traits, Fleet Construction activation, automatic Planet Fall allocation, fixed raid identity, defensive settlement and Phase0. Update source app/templates/briefings consistently after that draft is checked. This does not resume suspended Dessica.

Retain d20 systems with2–4 holdings averaging3; all planets/stations eligible for replacement Capital; lifespan guidance beside Manpower; fixed raider; survivor/reserve formation series. Phase0 remains construction damage, surviving repair effects, Logistics, then event. Keep global Soulstorm difficulty, Sector and mod work explicitly deferred. AI probabilities do not predict human battles.

## 8. Finite validation, then one integrated comparison

First qualify the four bot corrections in saved cases: useful continuation after target changes; safe abandonment; repair versus restart; emergency preservation; Carrier selected when odds matter; Platform when damage matters; concentration when per-fleet losses matter; exclusion of unavailable fleets; threatened versus safe investment; voluntary-deficit protection; and each catalogue role's useful competing option. Tests should permit multiple sound choices, not force a predetermined construction.

Then propose one combined paired study with fixed rules and recorded random strategy assignments including trait-aware choices. Collect pace, defensive shortages, project completion/use, all routes, ordinary/prize encounters, events, all12traits and formation setup legality together. Use equal trait opportunities and distinguish alliances from individual victory. If assessing the five-success tolerance, design for36 comparable focal opportunities per trait; do not pool changed-rule historical games or reinterpret14 as36. Split execution into resumable blocks under the user's four-hour preference and estimate time from a pilot before launching.

Do not add an indefinite bot-perfection gate. If these representative decisions are sound and a balance issue remains, change the rule rather than continuing to tune bots to conceal it. None of this new work has been started by this recommendation document.
