Authority

Scope reconciled

Significant stale text removed from the current edition
Old trait yields, free Void expansion, Swift expansion bonus, old Siege/Dread formulas, Grand Yard Major classification, generic construction prices, Transport +1/+2 victory return, no-combat Tender restriction, old +10 event dice modifiers, ambiguous Mobile contribution, and pending generation-table wording. No historical evidence was rewritten to imply it used the new values.

Important distinctions checked
Void Superiority is friendly Strength greater than hostile Strength; it is not absence of hostile fleets. Bombardment requires the latter. Human defending formations use defending fleet Strength, not holding defence. Actual host damage caps attached Integrity loss; Planet Fall collateral uses resolved assault damage including overkill. Fleet-category persistence does not make a Forge aboard a Mobile Capital a Fleet construction. Completed permanent strength capacity is a specific destruction exception. Income is capped before upkeep. Repair/regeneration is global Phase 0, not an action on the owner's turn.

Known implementation boundaries retained, not silently made into new rules
The selected simulator's default alliance function automatically allies non-Independent same-Alignment factions and has no dedicated hostile-override field. Atreus's explicitly approved Imperial infighting must be enforced by the campaign referee (and any future campaign-specific software), not assumed supported by this frozen study.
The generic human raid setup in the frozen engine names Iron Warriors. The reusable rule is the campaign's fixed raider; a different campaign must supply its own identity.
The inherited source requires Void Superiority for Fleet Merge; the selected engine's action generator omits that gate. The source requirement is preserved. This is a simulator coverage discrepancy, not approval to merge under hostile superiority.
The engine permits an assault declaration whose entire group is projected to die to cannons, spending the action and Dread charge then cancelling before ordinary attack costs. The source's minimum-five rule governs an assault that actually reaches commitment; this exceptional failed approach is recorded below the combat rule.
Scuttle safety must include mandatory destruction penalties; voluntary scuttling cannot create a resource deficit. The engine also permits Mobile scuttling subject to its full penalties; the general source now describes this explicitly.
Soulstorm global difficulty affects allied AI as well as enemies. This remains deferred. AI results are not human win-rate predictions. Sector rules remain deferred.
