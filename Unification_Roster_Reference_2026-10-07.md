# Unification roster and campaign representation reference

Researched 7 October 2026. Applies to Soulstorm Skirmish battles used by the meta campaign. Public release reference: Unification 7.3.8. The player's installed version and optional content have not been inspected; confirm the actual race menu when preparing a battle.

## Verified selectable roster

The team release page and official community wiki list 27 added races alongside Soulstorm's nine original races: 36 selections. The categories below describe the mod's roster, not campaign Alignments. A Minor using any of these armies remains an independent hostile Minor with no Alignment. [Team Unification release page](https://www.moddb.com/mods/unification-mod-dawn-of-war-soulstorm/), [official wiki race index](https://dawn-of-war-unification-mod.fandom.com/wiki/Races).

| Roster group | Original Soulstorm selections | Added Unification selections |
|---|---|---|
| Human / Imperial institutions | Imperial Guard; Sisters of Battle | Adeptus Mechanicus Explorators; Death Korps of Krieg; Praetorian Guard; Steel Legion; Vostroyan Firstborn; Witch Hunters |
| Astartes | Space Marines | 13th Company; Black Templars; Blood Angels; Dark Angels; Daemon Hunters / Inquisition Daemonhunt; Imperial Fists / Siege Vanguard; Legion of the Damned; Raven Guard; Salamanders |
| Chaos | Chaos Marines | Chaos Daemons; Death Guard; Emperor's Children; Fallen Angels; Night Lords; Renegade Guard; Thousand Sons; World Eaters |
| Xenos | Dark Eldar; Eldar; Necrons; Orks; Tau Empire | Farsight Enclaves; Harlequins; Tyranids; Ynnari |

Names have minor variations between the team page and wiki. Record the label shown in the installed menu in the battle setup. These are full gameplay rosters, not interchangeable cosmetic skins. Availability does not establish equal difficulty or balanced matchups.

## Human armies need not all use Imperial Guard

The following applications are campaign-authoring recommendations, rather than claims that the mod contains the named local factions.

| Selection | Relevant roster characteristics | Useful campaign representation |
|---|---|---|
| Imperial Guard | Conventional infantry, support weapons and armour | Naval landing forces, colonial PDF or general combined-arms armies |
| Death Korps of Krieg | Entrenched infantry, bunker infrastructure and vehicle support | Siege garrisons and militarised muster worlds; the local soldiers need not literally come from Krieg |
| Steel Legion | Mechanised forces; Armoured Fist and Tank Company branches | Armoured depot commands and mobile industrial armies |
| Vostroyan Firstborn | Firstborn infantry, heavy troops and Hussars among its units | Hereditary household troops, heirloom equipment and remount formations |
| Praetorian Guard | Regulars, irregulars, convicts, artillery and Ork mercenary options | Estate armies or human privateers employing mixed hired muscle |
| Renegade Guard | Vraksian Renegade Militia and Tekarn Shogunate branches | Industrial rebels, breakaway military formations and irregular warlords |
| Witch Hunters | Religious and enforcement forces, including Arbites | Shrine authorities or archive-security establishments; this is not a standalone Arbites race |
| Adeptus Mechanicus Explorators | Technical armies and machine-heavy forces | Reclamation guilds, augmented plant guards and independent technical enclaves |

Sources: [Krieg](https://dawn-of-war-unification-mod.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Korps_of_Krieg), [Steel Legion](https://dawn-of-war-unification-mod.fandom.com/wiki/Steel_Legion), [Vostroyans](https://dawn-of-war-unification-mod.fandom.com/wiki/Vostroyan_Firstborn), [Praetorians](https://dawn-of-war-unification-mod.fandom.com/wiki/Praetorian_Guard), [Renegade Guard](https://dawn-of-war-unification-mod.fandom.com/wiki/Renegade_Guard), [Witch Hunter Arbites](https://dawn-of-war-unification-mod.fandom.com/wiki/Witch_Hunters%3A_Arbites_Enforcers), [Mechanicus Explorators](https://dawn-of-war-unification-mod.fandom.com/wiki/Adeptus_Mechanicus_Explorators).

## Branches, optional content and practical limits

- Renegade Guard's two branches are not two separate race-menu entries. The wiki describes an AI choice with matchup-dependent weighting. Do not promise that an AI opponent will use only Vraksian or only Tekarn units unless the installed setup provides a verified way to enforce it.
- Steel Legion also contains branches. A narrative preference for tanks or mechanised infantry does not guarantee the AI's branch or purchases.
- Praetorian Guard includes Ork mercenary units. Allow for that when choosing it for a nominally human faction; do not promise a human-only enemy army that an uncontrolled AI may contradict.
- Optional win conditions can add or exchange units. Custodes are described as a Space Marine option, not an additional standalone race in the verified 36. Krieg's optional Cathay content is likewise not another roster entry. Record enabled options separately. [Game rules](https://dawn-of-war-unification-mod.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Rules), [Krieg options](https://dawn-of-war-unification-mod.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Korps_of_Krieg).
- Iron Warriors are not a standalone entry in the verified list. Use Chaos Marines as their declared representation unless the player's installed version/addon supplies a confirmed dedicated option. A Legion name alone is not evidence of a selectable race.
- Do not mistake announced future content or the separate expanded campaign addon for the installed Skirmish roster. [Expanded campaign overview](https://dawn-of-war-unification-mod.fandom.com/wiki/Expanded_Unification_Campaign).
- Choose a complete available roster and accept its gameplay. Do not quietly impose unit bans, invented bonuses or extra difficulty modifiers to make every unit match the fiction. Describe material differences as representation, and obtain a player ruling if one makes the intended battle unsuitable.

The Nail-Takers raider detachment uses Chaos Marines to represent Iron Warriors unless a dedicated installed option is confirmed. These representation changes do not alter meta-campaign traits, costs, fleet strengths, diplomacy, team counts or map-capacity rules. They do change actual RTS matchups: record the selected race, options, difficulty, map, teams and player-reported result so playtesting can reveal any problematic pairing. Simulator campaign win rates do not establish human Unification matchup difficulty.

## Use for future campaigns

Start from each faction's people, equipment, doctrine and terrain, then compare several available rosters. Use the entire roster when it suits the campaign; do not default every human to Guard or assume every Minor must be human. Repeated selections are acceptable when deliberate, but review the overall mix before publishing. Keep the fictional faction identity, exact selectable race, optional branches/content and campaign Alignment status as separate facts. Recheck the official roster after a mod update.
